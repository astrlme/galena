---
title: Schedule maintenance
description: Announce planned work that starts and completes on its own.
---

<Steps>
<Step>

### Schedule the window

In **Maintenance**, choose **Schedule window**:

- **Title**: what people see, such as "Database upgrade".
- **Starts (UTC)** and **Ends (UTC)**: the window must end after it starts. Allow some slack at
  the end; finishing early is better news than overrunning.
- **Message**: what will happen and what people may notice.
- **Components under maintenance**.

Subscribers following those components hear about it at once.

</Step>
<Step>

### Let it run

At the start time, the window moves to In progress, its components show Maintenance, and
subscribers are told. At the end time it completes, the components return to what their monitors
and incidents say, and subscribers are told again. You don't need to do anything.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Changing plans

- **Edit** a window any time before it completes. To end a running window early, set its end
  time to now; to extend it, move the end later.
- **Cancel** a window that hasn't started. It completes at once and subscribers hear it was
  cancelled.

## When it goes wrong

Monitors keep checking during a window, but their transitions are recorded as suppressed. If the
work causes a real outage, extend the window or publish an incident: the window only covers what
you announced.
