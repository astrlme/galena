---
title: Approvals in Slack
description: Approve or dismiss a monitor's draft from a Slack channel, and see what's open with /incident.
---

When a monitor whose publish policy is **Ask a person first** goes down, Galena opens a draft and
waits for someone before it tells your customers. With the Slack app, that draft also arrives as
a card in a Slack channel, with **Approve and publish** and **Dismiss** buttons. The card states its
deadline: the draft publishes on its own at that time if the monitor is still down, and is
dismissed if it has recovered.

This is a different thing from [Slack notifications](/docs/guides/slack/), which post what your
status page publishes through an incoming webhook. You can use both.

Each deployment uses a Slack app of its own, because Slack sends button presses to your
deployment's address.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create the Slack app

From the repository root, print the app's manifest:

```sh
pnpm galena slack-manifest
```

In Slack, go to [api.slack.com/apps](https://api.slack.com/apps), choose **Create New App →
From a manifest**, pick your Slack workspace, and paste it. The manifest sets the request URLs to
your dashboard's address and asks for these permissions:

| Scope | Why |
| --- | --- |
| `chat:write`, `chat:write.public` | Post approval cards, also to a public channel the app hasn't joined |
| `commands` | `/incident` |
| `users:read`, `users:read.email` | Check that whoever presses a button is a Galena member, by their Slack email |
| `incoming-webhook` | Ask, while installing, which channel approval cards go to |

</Step>
<Step>

### Store the app's secrets

On the app's **Basic Information** page, copy the client ID, client secret and signing secret
into one SecureString, as JSON:

```sh
aws ssm put-parameter --region <home region> --type SecureString \
  --name /galena/<name>/slack-app \
  --value '{"clientId":"…","clientSecret":"…","signingSecret":"…"}'
```

The API reads it when it starts, so store it before you deploy; an API that's already running
picks it up with your next upgrade. Without this parameter, Slack stays off and its addresses
answer 404.

</Step>
<Step>

### Connect Slack

In **Settings → Slack**, an admin chooses **Connect Slack**. Slack asks which channel approval
cards go to; choose it and allow. Back in Settings, the card names your Slack workspace and the
channel.

**Disconnect Slack** forgets the connection and revokes the app's token in Slack.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Who can decide a draft

Galena matches the Slack user who presses a button to a member of your Galena workspace by their
Slack email. Only editors, admins and owners can decide; the audit log records that member as
the one who published or dismissed it. Anyone else gets a reply only they can see, and the draft
stays as it was:

- a Slack email that isn't a member's: "Galena doesn't know your Slack email";
- a viewer: "viewers can't publish or dismiss";
- someone from another organisation's Slack, in a channel shared through Slack Connect: "only
  people in this Slack workspace can publish or dismiss it". Their email is set by their own
  Slack admin, so Galena doesn't match it;
- a draft someone already decided, in Slack or in the dashboard, or that published at its
  deadline: "This draft was already published or dismissed".

Once someone decides in Slack, the card changes to say who did it and when, and the monitor's
own wait ends at once instead of at the deadline.

## `/incident`

`/incident` replies, only to you, with every open incident and every draft waiting for
approval, newest first, each linking to the dashboard. Drafts get the same two buttons. It works
for members only.

## When Aurora is paused

Slack expects an answer within 3 seconds, and the database can take about 15 to resume after a
quiet period. Galena answers Slack before it touches the database and does the rest in the
background, so a press doesn't wait for it; the card updates once the database is back.

## When something goes wrong

- **Settings says "Couldn't connect Slack"**: check the three values in
  `/galena/<name>/slack-app`, then connect again.
- **No card arrives in a private channel**: invite the app to the channel (`/invite @Galena`).
  Public channels don't need it.
- **A card still shows its buttons after the draft was decided in the dashboard or at its
  deadline**: cards change only when someone presses them. A press then says, only to you, that
  the draft was already decided; the dashboard shows what happened.
