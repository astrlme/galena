title: Components and groups
description: The things your users care about, and where their status comes from.
---

A **component** is something your users recognise and care about: "API", "Dashboard",
"Webhooks", "EU region". It is what the status page lists, what incidents and maintenance
windows name, and what subscribers can choose to hear about.

Components describe your product from the outside. A database or a queue is usually not a
component; the feature that breaks when it fails is.

## Groups

A **group** puts related components under one heading on the page, such as "Europe" and "North
America". Components and groups each have an order, and the page follows it. Move them up or
down in **Components** in the dashboard.

A component can sit outside any group. Deleting a group keeps its components and moves them out
of it.

## Where a component's status comes from

A component has one of five statuses:

| Status | Label on the page |
| --- | --- |
| `operational` | Operational |
| `degraded_performance` | Degraded performance |
| `partial_outage` | Partial outage |
| `major_outage` | Major outage |
| `under_maintenance` | Maintenance |

The values are the same as Atlassian Statuspage's. Galena works the status out from four
sources, in this order:

<Diagram name="component-status" />

1. **A manual status**, when one is set, wins over everything else.
2. **An active maintenance window** that names the component shows Maintenance.
3. Otherwise the component shows the **worst opinion** among:
   - each attached monitor's state (see [mapping](/docs/concepts/detection/#from-monitor-state-to-component-status)),
   - each open, published incident that names it, with the status the incident gives it.
4. When nothing has an opinion (no monitors yet, no open incidents), the component keeps the
   status it had.

Resolving an incident removes its say at once. Draft, dismissed and internal incidents never
change a component's status on the page.

<Callout>
A manual status is part of the data model and the computation, but the dashboard can't set one
yet.
</Callout>
