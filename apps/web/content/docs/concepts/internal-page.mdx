---
title: Internal page
description: The status page as members see it, behind sign-in.
---

The **internal page** is the status page as the people of your workspace see it. It shows
everything the public page shows, and also:

- the status that [internal-only monitors](/docs/concepts/monitors/#publish-policy) give their
  components, and
- incidents posted with **Internal page only**, which nobody is notified about.

It lives on the dashboard's address, at `/internal/`. Open it from **Internal page** in the
dashboard's menu: that lets your browser in for 12 hours, and signing out ends it at once.
Bookmark the menu link rather than the page itself: once the 12 hours are up, the page answers
403 until you open it from the dashboard again.

## How it keeps working

Like the public page, it is static. Each publish writes its files next to the public page's, and
CloudFront serves them from the same buckets, falling back to the replica region. A browser that
was let in keeps reading it while the API and the database are down.

CloudFront checks a signed cookie on every request. The API signs it, after a member signs in,
with a key that the first deploy makes and keeps as an SSM SecureString; CloudFront holds only the
public half. API keys can't open the page.

## Limits

- The 90-day history and uptime percentages are the public page's: internal-only monitors change
  a component's current status, not its history.
- Someone removed from the workspace can read the page until their 12 hours run out.
- It has no feeds and no email subscriptions, since a feed reader can't sign in.
